ایوان
ay·vān · Persian · noun

The room that is half house, half garden.

In Persian architecture the ayvan is the deep, roofed porch between the rooms and the courtyard. It is where the family sits in the evening, in the shade, facing the water.

The whole villa is drawn around that idea. The roof reaches 4 m past the glass, so every ground-floor room has its own ayvan.
